The Coffee Gap

Comparing the World's Coffee Harvest with Expert Ratings

Almost half of the World's Coffee comes from two Countries

Brazil and Vietnam alone grow about 49% of the world's coffee.

Most other producers are far smaller.

Circle area shows tonnes produced in 2024. Hover over a circle for the figure.

Small countries grow the most coffee per person

Honduras, Laos and Nicaragua produce the most coffee per person.

Countries that grow no coffee are shaded beige, not left blank.

Darker green means more kilograms of coffee per person in 2024. Hover over a country for its value.

Brazil outgrows its closest rivals combined

Brazil harvested about 3.4 million tonnes in 2024 which is more than Vietnam and Colombia combined.

Output drops below 0.8 million tonnes from Indonesia onward.

Bars are sorted by tonnes produced. Hover over a bar for the figure.

The fastest growers are not the biggest producers

Uganda is up about 80% since 2014, while Brazil is up about 20%.

Colombia is the least stable, dipping to about 77 in 2021.

Each line is indexed so that 2014 equals 100. So a value of 150 means 50% more than in 2014.

Most coffees score 90 points or higher

About 80% of the 9,003 scored coffees receive 90 points or more.

The most common score is 93.

Each bar counts the reviews that received one score out of 100.

Reviewed coffees are getting lighter in roast

Medium-Light roasts led from 2013 to 2023.

Light roasts now hold the largest share in 2024 to 2026, though 2026 is a partial year.

Each cell shows the share of that year's reviews with one roast level. Darker means a bigger share.

Origin moves ratings by only a few points

Median ratings run from about 91 (Brazil and Mexico) to about 94 (Panama).

Brazil has one of the lowest medians.

Each box covers the middle half of ratings and the line inside marks the median. Only origins with 100 or more reviews are shown.

Big producers get few reviews

Brazil and Vietnam grow nearly half the world's coffee but get under 3% of the reviews.

Ethiopia grows about 5% of the world's coffee but receives roughly 23% of the reviews.

Brown dots show share of production and orange dots show share of reviews. A longer line means a bigger gap.

Big producers rank low on quality

Six of the eight biggest producers rank in the bottom half for quality.

Vietnam and Uganda are not ranked because they have too few reviews.

Each line joins a country's production rank to its quality rank, where 1 is the highest. Hover over a line to highlight a country.

Most reviewed coffees are roasted in two places

93% of reviews for these six origins were roasted in the United States or Taiwan.

The lines show where reviewed coffees were grown and roasted, not trade volumes.

Line width shows the number of reviews. Hover over a line to highlight it.
